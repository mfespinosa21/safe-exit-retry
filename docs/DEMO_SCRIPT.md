## Final 30 seconds - What changed my mind this week

### 3:00-3:30

**On screen:** Keep the final result visible or turn the camera toward yourself.

**Say:**

> What changed my mind this week is that realism is not the most important part of a simulation. At first, I focused on making the emergency experience feel convincing. The Adversary work showed me that the more convincing a simulation looks, the easier it is to confuse simulated performance with real readiness. My final position is that simulation is useful only when it produces a specific human-owned correction, changes the next rehearsal, and clearly limits what the result can claim.
